FAQ

Everything guests ask before booking

Sample answers based on typical homestay questions — edit freely.

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 1:00 PM and check-out by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are offered free of charge when the room allows — just ask on WhatsApp the day before.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Yes — on this sample site, breakfast for two plus evening chai are included with every room. Other meals can be added at The Verandah or as in-room dining.

How do I book, and do I pay online?

Booking here is conversational: message us on WhatsApp or the enquiry form, we confirm availability, and we send a simple secure payment link. There is no heavy booking engine — a human replies within minutes (sample promise).

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation until 72 hours before check-in on sample rates; after that, one night is charged. Holiday-season bookings (Dec 20 – Jan 5) carry a 7-day window. Full details on the Policies page.

Are children and pets welcome?

Children of all ages are welcome, with bunk nooks and activity hampers in the Family Cottage. Well-behaved pets are welcome in the Garden Cottage only — mention them while booking.

Is the property suitable for a workation?

Yes — high-speed Wi-Fi reaches every room and the verandah has plug-and-work seating. Most guests stop answering emails by day two anyway.

How far are restaurants and shops?

Madikeri town with restaurants, pharmacies and markets is about 20 minutes by road. We're happy to arrange a driver or deliver dinner to your room.

What is the best time to visit?

September to March is the classic window — misty mornings and green hills. Monsoon (June–August) has its own dramatic beauty and lower sample rates.

Still Unsure?

A human replies in minutes

No bots, no ticket numbers. Message us and we'll answer anything the FAQ missed — including whether the pool is warm in December.
