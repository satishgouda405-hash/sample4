Welcome to the ridge

Nine rooms. One valley.
Zero noise but the birds.

Kanaka Ridge Retreat is a sample boutique homestay carved into a working coffee estate, Cloudfost hideaways above the Coorg valley. It is built for couples, small families and slow travellers who want nature with genuine comfort — not a hotel dropped in a jungle.

Every room faces the valley. Every meal leans on our own garden. Every evening ends at the fire.
